Declare a document's fonts as a font manifest and report every face that is missing. `StyleSheet.fonts` is now a record keyed by font name: an app font with its `FontDeclaration` faces (`weight`, `style`, `unicodeRange`, `script: 'hebrew'`), or a local font with its fallback. A Hebrew face with no range covers `U+0590-05FF,U+FB1D-FB4F`. A declared face the host does not supply is reported `unsupplied`, and one Chromium does not load is reported `unloaded`; composition continues at the fallback. Render reports through `fontLoad.report({ failures })`, and the page, paper, stage and book elements dispatch `fontfailure`. Add `matchFaces`, `rangeSample`, `fontManifestWithReferences` and `persistedFontManifestWithReferences`. Breaking: the retired `FontReference[]` form of `style.fonts` is refused, and a font the source does not know no longer refuses the render.
