Compose books and printed pages with one measure, plan, and dress runtime. Replace `BOOK_COMPOSER_SCRIPT` and `PRINTED_PAGE_COMPOSER_SCRIPT` with `COMPOSER_SCRIPT` and `composerScript`, and add optional `PaginateBlock.id`, `PaginateBlock.breakBefore` and `PaginateBlock.breakAfter` measurements. A forced page break before or after a block starts a new page in the plan.
